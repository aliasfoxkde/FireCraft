Workspace captures

The editor, as it
actually renders.

Every image below carries its provenance. Captures are labelled with the build and date they came from; concept artwork is labelled as concept artwork so it is never mistaken for a screenshot.

i

How captures are handled. Screenshots come from identified builds and real workflows. When a capture is older than the current release it stays visible only with its build date, so the site never implies that an old workspace is today's workspace. Concept art is never presented as a capture.

Want your artwork here? Open an issue with the document and a description of the workflow — captures of real projects are more useful than empty canvases.
