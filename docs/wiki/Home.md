## How to read the roadmap

The [Parity Roadmap](Parity-Roadmap) separates unfinished Fireworks CS6 behavior and presentation from newer FireCraft additions. It is an ordered summary of the known work, not a claim that every undocumented CS6 behavior has been inventoried. For the detailed phase plan and dated receipts, see [NEXT.md](https://github.com/aliasfoxkde/FireCraft/blob/main/docs/NEXT.md) and [PROGRESS.md](https://github.com/aliasfoxkde/FireCraft/blob/main/docs/PROGRESS.md).

> **Clean-room boundary:** FireCraft is independent of Adobe. Its behavior references documentation and observation; Adobe source code and artwork are not included.
